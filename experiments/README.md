# Experiments

This folder contains focused experiments, prototypes, and technical investigations.

## What Belongs Here

- Small explorations of ideas or architectures
- Proof-of-concept work with clear scope
- Notes that explain what was tried and what was learned

## What Does Not Belong Here

- Polished demos intended for presentation
- Production-grade tools
- Large, open-ended sandboxes without clear purpose

## Naming

Use lowercase kebab-case for experiment folders. Keep names short and descriptive, for example `rag-evaluation-baseline`.

## Typical Entry

An experiment folder should usually contain:

- a local `README.md`
- the minimal files needed to reproduce or review the experiment
- a short note on scope, status, or findings
